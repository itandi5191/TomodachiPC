# 🏝️ TomodachiPC - Manage your virtual island life easily

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-TomodachiPC-blue.svg)](https://github.com/itandi5191/TomodachiPC)

TomodachiPC brings your favorite island simulation to your Windows desktop. This application lets you manage your Mii characters, design apartments, and track daily island events. You oversee every aspect of your virtual community through an intuitive interface. The software includes tools to edit personalities, organize gift systems, and catalog your food preferences database. You gain full control over island news, dream collections, and character interactions.

## 🎮 Core Features

This software includes tools to enhance your island experience:

* Mii Personality Maker: Define character traits, hobbies, and distinct quirks for every resident on your island.
* Apartment Editor: Customize living spaces, choose room themes, and arrange furniture to fit your style.
* Food Preferences Database: Track what your Miis like to eat to increase their happiness levels.
* Gift System: Manage inventory and send items to your Miis to strengthen their bonds.
* Interaction Logs: View detailed reports of conversations and friendships between your island inhabitants.
* Voice Synthesizer: Adjust pitch, speed, and tone for your Mii character voices.
* QR Code Gallery: Import or export Mii designs using standard image formats.
* Island News Tracker: Monitor daily events, island rankings, and special seasonal happenings.
* Dream Collection: Save and view unique dreams experienced by your islanders.

## ⚙️ Using the Interface

The main dashboard provides a view of your entire island. You can switch between different tabs to manage specific features. The sidebar offers quick access to your inventory, character lists, and settings. 

To add a new character, click the plus icon in the character tab. Enter their name, birthday, and personality type. Assign them to an apartment to include them in the island population. 

To manage island news, check the updates tab. You can mark events as read or add custom reminders for specific days. If you need to change settings, click the gear icon in the top right corner. Here you can adjust volume, screen resolution, and interface colors.

## 📁 Managing Mii Data

The software saves all your data in a local folder on your computer. You can back up your progress by copying the data folder to an external drive or a cloud storage service. 

To generate a new Mii character, use the built-in generator. Pick facial features, clothing, and voice settings. You can save these characters to your local gallery for future use. The software supports importing QR codes. Save the QR code image to your computer, then select the import button in the gallery tab to add the character to your roster.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the software fails to launch, verify that you have the latest drivers for your graphics card. You can download these from your computer manufacturer's website. If you encounter missing files, restart the installation process. 

Sometimes, security software might block the application. You can add an exception in your antivirus settings to allow the executable to run. Ensure your Windows system is fully updated to prevent compatibility conflicts. If the window appears blank, reduce your screen display scaling settings in the Windows Control Panel to 100 percent.

## 📝 Performance Tips

Keep your island running fast by cleaning up old interaction logs. You can delete outdated reports in the settings menu. Limit the number of background applications running while you use the software to save memory. 

If you notice stuttering during voice playback, close other media players or internet browsers. Using a wired internet connection helps if you download extra content or sync with online galleries.
